How the Process Works

With Deepu Siva handling the entire process for you

1

Free Site Assessment

Our engineer visits your home to assess roof space, orientation, and shadow-free area. We calculate the ideal system size for your electricity usage.

Done by Deepu Siva — Free
2

Portal Registration

We assist you in applying on pmsuryaghar.gov.in using your Aadhaar, electricity consumer number, and bank account details.

Required before installation
3

Vendor Approval

Your DISCOM (TANGEDCO) approves the installation. Deepu Siva Pvt Ltd is an empanelled vendor — this step is handled automatically by us.

Handled by us
4

Solar Panel Installation

Our certified team installs your solar panels, mounting structure, inverter, and wiring. Typically completed in 1–2 days.

5-Year Workmanship Warranty
5

Net Meter & Inspection

TANGEDCO installs a net meter and inspects the system. This may take 15–30 days after installation.

We follow up with TANGEDCO
6

Subsidy to Bank Account

After inspection approval, the government transfers the subsidy amount directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT within 30 days.

₹30,000 – ₹78,000 directly credited

Who is Eligible?

Indian residential homeowners with a valid TANGEDCO connection

Apartment owners (with society approval for rooftop access)

Farmers with residential connection (separate from agricultural meter)

Commercial / industrial / factory connections (HT consumers)
